The innovation

A turbine built for real-world wind.

Most small turbines are designed for strong winds. Ours is designed for the gentle breezes that blow most of the time — and keeps performing as the wind picks up.

Variable-geometry VAWT

Two turbines in one.

Our patented vertical-axis design changes the way its blades meet the wind, moving from drag to lift as the wind speed increases.

🌬️
Low wind: drag mode
In light winds the blades behave like a Savonius rotor, catching the wind like a cup. This gives strong starting torque, so the turbine starts turning early and generates for more hours of the day.
⚡
Stronger wind: lift mode
As the wind builds, the blades move into a Darrieus-style lift configuration — like an aircraft wing — for higher efficiency and stronger output across real-world wind conditions.
The early-start advantage

More hours generating, not just higher peaks.

Traditional small turbines only start producing useful power at 3–4 m/s. The Aventus turbine is designed to cut in at around 2–2.5 m/s and ramp up steeply from there.

Across much of South Africa, the wind blows at 2–5 m/s for a large part of the year. Capturing that band is where the real energy is — consistency beats peak performance.

How it was developed

Engineered from first principles.

🛠️
Hands-on prototypes
Several working prototypes have been designed and built in-house, each refining the blades, rotor and mechanism.
🌀
Own wind tunnel
Blade shapes are tested and optimised in our own wind tunnel, resulting in a custom aerofoil tuned for this design.
📐
Built for manufacture
Simple, straight, constant-section blades and a sturdy lattice tower keep the design robust, affordable and locally manufacturable.
