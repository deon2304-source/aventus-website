The product

The Aventus Aero Power Tower

A modular hybrid tower that puts solar panels and our low-wind vertical turbine on one compact structure — a complete off-grid power plant on a single footprint.

Modular by design

Stack it to suit the site.

  • 3 m lattice modulesA sturdy, tank-stand-style steel structure built in stackable 3 m sections.
  • Solar on every moduleEach module carries a pair of panels on opposite sides, angled at about 30° for year-round sun.
  • Turbine on topThe Aventus vertical turbine sits in an open 4.5 m cage at the top, where the wind is strongest.
  • Taller = more powerMore modules lift the turbine into better wind and add more solar panels. A typical three-module tower stands around 15 m tall.
