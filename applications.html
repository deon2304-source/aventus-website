Applications

Power where it matters most.

The Power Tower is designed for places where the grid is absent, unreliable or too expensive to extend. Here is how we see it making a difference.

Application

Powering Africa’s food security

Off-grid, sustainable energy for irrigation pumps, boreholes and cold storage — unlocking higher yields and year-round productivity on every rural hectare.
