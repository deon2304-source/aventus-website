The problem

Solar stops at sunset.
Diesel never stops costing.

Solar power is driving Africa’s move to off-grid energy — but it only works while the sun shines. At night and during storms, sites fall back on batteries or expensive, noisy diesel generators.

Meanwhile, conventional wind turbines are built for strong winds and sit idle in the gentle 2–5 m/s breezes that make up most of South Africa’s real wind.

Our answer

One tower. Wind and sun, around the clock.

  • 24/7 generationSolar produces by day; the Aventus turbine keeps producing at night and in poor weather.
  • Built for low windOur turbine starts turning at around 2–2.5 m/s — earlier than conventional designs.
  • One footprintWind and solar share a single, compact tower, cutting space and installation cost.
  • No fuel, low maintenanceClean, quiet generation that replaces costly diesel.
